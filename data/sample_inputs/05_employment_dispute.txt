                              Plaintiff,

                              Defendant.

-------------------------------------------------------------
COMPLAINT FOR WRONGFUL TERMINATION AND RETALIATION
-------------------------------------------------------------

DOCUMENT TYPE: Civil Complaint (Employment)

PARTIES:
  Plaintiff:  Priya Ramesh, an individual residing in Cook County, Illinois,
              formerly employed as a Senior Data Engineer.
  Defendant:  Heliodon Software, Inc., a Delaware corporation headquartered
              in Chicago, Illinois.

STATEMENT OF FACTS:

1. Plaintiff was hired by Defendant on June 1, 2019, as a Senior Data
   Engineer at an annual salary of $148,000, with a written offer letter
   describing a 90-day severance entitlement upon termination without cause.

2. On August 12, 2021, Plaintiff submitted a written internal complaint to
   Human Resources alleging that Defendant was billing a government client
   for hours not actually worked.

3. On September 3, 2021, three weeks after the complaint, Plaintiff received
   her first-ever negative performance review after two years of "Exceeds
   Expectations" ratings.

4. On November 19, 2021, Defendant terminated Plaintiff's employment,
   citing "restructuring," though her team of six engineers was retained and
   a replacement was hired in January 2022.

5. Defendant refused to pay the 90-day severance described in the offer
   letter, asserting the termination was "for cause."

RELIEF SOUGHT:
   Plaintiff seeks back pay, the contractual severance of $36,500, statutory
   damages under the Illinois Whistleblower Act, reinstatement or front pay,
   and reasonable attorneys' fees.
