MERIDIAN LOGISTICS, INC.,
                              Plaintiff,
        v.

                              Defendant.

-------------------------------------------------------------
CASE SUMMARY
-------------------------------------------------------------

DOCUMENT TYPE: Complaint for Breach of Contract

PARTIES:
  Plaintiff:  Meridian Logistics, Inc., a Delaware corporation with
              its principal place of business in San Jose, California.
  Defendant:  Northgate Packaging Solutions, LLC, a California limited
              liability company based in Fremont, California.

STATEMENT OF FACTS:

1. On January 15, 2021, Meridian Logistics and Northgate Packaging
   entered into a written Supply Agreement under which Northgate agreed
   to deliver 50,000 corrugated shipping containers per month at a
   fixed price of $2.40 per unit.

2. The dispute began in March 2021 when Northgate delivered only 32,000
   units in the month of March, falling 18,000 units short of the
   contractual minimum.

3. On April 2, 2021, Meridian sent a written notice of breach demanding
   that Northgate cure the shortfall within thirty (30) days.

4. Northgate failed to cure. In May 2021, deliveries fell further to
   21,000 units, and on June 10, 2021 Northgate notified Meridian that
   it would suspend all shipments indefinitely due to a raw-material
   shortage.

5. As a direct result of the suspension, Meridian was forced to procure
   replacement containers on the open market at $3.85 per unit,
   incurring approximately $1.2 million in cover damages.

RELIEF SOUGHT:
   Plaintiff seeks compensatory damages of not less than $1,200,000,
   pre-judgment interest, and costs of suit.
